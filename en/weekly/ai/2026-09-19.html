AI September 19, 2026

UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-09-19

Overview

The week of September 12–19, 2026 was dominated by UNESCO's 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, co-hosted with Saudi Arabia in Riyadh (September 14–17). UNESCO reported more than 6,300 participants and over 50 Member State delegations, and launched new implementation tools including an AI, environment and ecosystems toolkit and RAM 2.0, an enhanced version of its Readiness Assessment Methodology. Ahead of the UN General Assembly (UNGA81), UNESCO outlined its AI priorities as co-chair of the UN Dialogue on AI Governance. Other items included a UNESCO Campus event on AI and learning for 700 students, AI-ethics engagement by Beruniy Prize laureates, and a new UNESCO–Mexico agreement covering AI and children's learning.

Global cooperation for ethical AI governance reinforced at UNESCO's 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of AI in Saudi Arabia

Date: September 18, 2026 (forum held September 14–17, 2026)
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-cooperation-ethical-ai-governance-reinforced-unescos-4th-global-forum-ethics-ai-saudi-arabia

UNESCO and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, represented by the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) and the International Centre for AI Research and Ethics (ICAIRE), co-hosted the forum in Riyadh. UNESCO reported "over 6,300 participants and more than 50 Member States delegations." Tools launched included an "AI, environment and ecosystems toolkit," RAM 2.0 (described as an enhanced, more action-oriented version of the Readiness Assessment Methodology), a meta-analysis of 55 country reports, and AI ethics action cohorts with 17 participants from 16 countries.

Learning with new technologies and artificial intelligence

Date: September 16, 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/learning-new-technologies-and-artificial-intelligence

A UNESCO Campus event at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris brought together 700 students to discuss how digital tools and AI affect learning. Speakers included Margarida Romero of the Institute for Research in Artificial Intelligence (IIIA-CSIC) and the founder of the Laboratory for Innovation and Digital Technology in Education (LINE). They argued that AI should amplify human intention rather than replace the effort of learning, and they stressed critical thinking about social media, where, one speaker noted, "misinformation travels seven times faster." TECH4ALL by Huawei was the lead partner, and the article concludes that "the future of education will not be written by algorithms alone."

UNESCO calls for global cooperation to address current global challenges of Artificial Intelligence and Ocean Science at UNGA

Date: September 18, 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-calls-global-cooperation-address-current-global-challenges-artificial-intelligence-and-ocean

Ahead of UNGA81's High-Level Week, UNESCO set out its agenda as co-chair of the UN Dialogue on AI Governance, stating that AI should "serve human intelligence, human dignity and human agency — not weaken or replace them." Scheduled UNESCO events included Digital@UNGA 2026 (September 21) and a Coalition for Children's Rights and Protection in the Age of AI (September 22). The article also says UNESCO "is expected to sign" institutional cooperation agreements with the UNFCCC and the UN Alliance of Civilizations (UNAOC) during UNGA.

UNESCO-Uzbekistan Beruniy Prize laureates highlight ethics of AI in Kazakhstan

Date: September 18, 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-uzbekistan-beruniy-prize-laureates-highlight-ethics-ai-kazakhstan

Beruniy Prize laureates Professor Virgílio Almeida and Professor Liang Zheng took part in the third International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence in Astana, Kazakhstan, on August 5–6, 2026. They led discussions on the ethics of AI, covering accountability, governance frameworks and responsible development, with more than 300 competitors and 200 international experts. The article was published this week, but it reports on an August event.

UNESCO reaffirms its commitment to working with Mexico and its people to address shared challenges

Date: September 12, 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-reaffirms-its-commitment-working-mexico-and-its-people-address-shared-challenges

UNESCO Assistant Director-General Lidia Brito visited Mexico. During the visit she marked the 60th anniversary of International Literacy Day in Chiapas and signed agreements on education technology and water security. On AI, a Memorandum of Understanding provides "technical support to the national education system" on "the use of technological devices, social media and artificial intelligence" to protect children's and young people's well-being and learning.
