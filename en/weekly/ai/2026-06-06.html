AI June 6, 2026

UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-06-06

Overview

During the week of 30 May – 6 June 2026, UNESCO advanced a range of artificial intelligence initiatives spanning digital governance, inclusive education, environmental sustainability, and internet governance. Key highlights include UNESCO's participation in EuroDIG 2026 in Brussels — where multi-stakeholder partnerships for inclusive digital futures were championed — a webinar on AI and inclusive education in Latin America and the Caribbean, the publication of UNESCO's announcement supporting the upcoming UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (6–7 July), and a joint ITU-UNESCO event on green digital transformation in Equatorial Guinea. These developments reflect UNESCO's continued work to ensure AI development is ethical, inclusive, rights-based, and environmentally sustainable.

UNESCO Advocates Multi-Stakeholder Partnerships for Inclusive Digital Futures at EuroDIG 2026

  • Date: 2026-06-05
  • Summary: UNESCO participated in the 2026 European Internet Governance Dialogue (EuroDIG), held on 26–27 May at the Charlemagne Building of the European Commission in Brussels under the theme "European Voices for the Future of the Internet." UNESCO's Director for Digital Inclusion, Policies and Transformation, Guilherme Canela, underscored the organization's support for the WSIS+20 Resolution and called for broader multi-stakeholder cooperation on digital governance and AI policy. UNESCO also marked the 25th anniversary of the Information for All Programme (IFAP) and advocated for inclusive, rights-based approaches to AI and multilingual internet access. EuroDIG 2026 concluded with calls for stronger digital inclusion and the participation of diverse voices in shaping AI policy.
  • Source: UNESCO – UNESCO advocates multi-stakeholder partnerships for inclusive digital futures at EuroDIG 2026

ITU and UNESCO Strengthen Collaboration on Green Digital Transformation in Equatorial Guinea

  • Date: 2026-06-05
  • Summary: UNESCO and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) advanced cooperation on green digital transformation following the 16th Symposium on Information and Communication Technologies, Environment, Climate Change and Circular Economy, held 19–22 May 2026 in Malabo, Equatorial Guinea. The event culminated in the adoption of the Malabo Declaration on sustainable digital transformation, climate action and youth empowerment, which links digital infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and circular-economy approaches to climate priorities across Africa. UNESCO's involvement underscores the organization's commitment to ensuring AI development minimizes environmental harm and supports sustainable development goals.
  • Source: UNESCO – ITU and UNESCO strengthen collaboration on green digital transformation in Equatorial Guinea

Webinar: Perspectives on Inclusive Education in the Age of AI – Latin America and the Caribbean

  • Date: 2026-06-03
  • Summary: UNESCO hosted a webinar titled "Perspectives on Inclusive Education in the Age of AI: Emerging Frameworks from Latin America and the Caribbean" on 3 June 2026, connected to its regional Observatory on Artificial Intelligence in Education launched in April 2026. The session brought together the region's 33 Ministries of Education, universities, research centers, and civil society partners to explore how AI can be deployed to advance equitable and quality education. The Observatory — the first such UN-anchored platform dedicated to AI in education for Latin America and the Caribbean — serves as the key vehicle for regional policy dialogue and data sharing on AI's role in learning.
  • Source: Webinar – Perspectives on Inclusive Education in the Age of AI: Emerging Frameworks from Latin America and the Caribbean (Indico.UN)

Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, 6–7 July 2026 (Announcement)

  • Date: 2026-06-02
  • Summary: UNESCO published an announcement supporting the first-ever UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance, scheduled for 6–7 July 2026 at Palexpo in Geneva, taking place alongside the WSIS Forum 2026 (6–10 July) and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit (7–10 July). The Dialogue — convened by the United Nations Secretary-General — is the multilateral platform where governments, the private sector, academia and civil society will convene to discuss international AI governance cooperation, share best practices, and pursue open, transparent and inclusive discussions. UNESCO is a key contributing partner, with early registration open through 25 June 2026.
  • Source: UNESCO – Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, 6–7 July
