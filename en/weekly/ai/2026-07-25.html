AI July 25, 2026

UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-07-25

Overview

The week of July 18–25, 2026 saw UNESCO's most significant AI milestone in the launch of the Global MOOC on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, a free online course developed in partnership with LG AI Research and unveiled on July 20 at the "Ethics by Design: Industry and Innovation" conference in Seoul. Hosted on Coursera, the course offers 10 modules across three learning tracks and is grounded in UNESCO's landmark 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI. Additional context from the preceding weeks includes the Caribbean AI Roadmap gaining regional backing (July 7) and the inaugural UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (July 6–7), both reflecting the continued global momentum on AI policy and ethics.

UNESCO and LG AI Research Launch Global MOOC on the Ethics of AI

Date: 20 July 2026
URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-and-lg-ai-research-launch-global-mooc-ethics-ai

UNESCO and LG AI Research officially launched the Global MOOC on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence at the "Ethics by Design: Industry and Innovation" conference held at the Korean National Commission for UNESCO premises in Seoul. The course, hosted free of charge on Coursera, features 10 modules across three learning tracks comprising over eight hours of self-paced content including video lessons, quizzes, and interactive exercises. It is grounded in the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI, unanimously adopted by UNESCO's 193 Member States in 2021 as the first global normative instrument on AI ethics. Designed for technologists, practitioners, researchers, students, and policymakers, the launch was attended by UNESCO Director-General Prof. Khaled El-Enany, Korean Deputy Prime Minister Dr. Kyung Hoon Bae, and LG AI Research Head Dr. Woohyung Lim. Plans for offline training pilots targeting local AI developers and researchers are scheduled for the second half of 2026.

UNESCO Caribbean AI Roadmap Wins Regional Backing

Date: 7 July 2026 (included as recent context)
URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence

UNESCO's Caribbean AI Roadmap received formal endorsement from regional stakeholders, marking a significant step in UNESCO's support for region-specific AI governance frameworks. The roadmap provides Caribbean nations with a structured approach to AI policy, governance, and capacity-building aligned with UNESCO's global AI ethics principles, and exemplifies the organization's ongoing effort to ensure that AI development benefits all regions, including the Global South.

UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance Opens in Geneva

Date: 6–7 July 2026 (included as recent context)
URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-dialogue-ai-governance-geneva-6-7-july

The first session of the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance, established by the UN General Assembly, was held at the Palexpo convention center in Geneva alongside the WSIS Forum 2026 and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit. Bringing together governments, private sector, academia, and civil society from around the world, the two-day event covered international cooperation, AI safety, interoperability, and capacity-building, with the Independent International Scientific Panel on AI presenting its preliminary assessment. An urgent call was issued for safe and inclusive AI that benefits all nations, with special emphasis on ensuring that the least technologically advanced countries are not left behind in shaping global AI governance.
