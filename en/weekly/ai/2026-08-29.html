Pilot Program Launches in Mexico to Co-Build a Model of Centers of Excellence Between UNESCO, CONALEP, and DGETI

  • Date: August 18, 2026
  • URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/pilot-program-launches-mexico-co-build-model-centers-excellence-between-unesco-conalep-and-dgeti
  • UNESCO, CONALEP, and DGETI launched a pilot initiative at eight technical education campuses in Mexico's Valley of Mexico region for 2026–2027. The program establishes innovation hubs to strengthen vocational education through digital talent development and teacher training. Lessons learned will inform scaling the model across Latin America and the Caribbean.

Paving the Way for Responsible AI: UNESCO and the G7 Toolkit Initiative

  • Date: August 15, 2026 (estimated)
  • URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/paving-way-responsible-ai-unesco-and-g7-toolkit-initiative
  • This article covers UNESCO's collaboration with G7 partners on a toolkit designed to support responsible AI implementation globally. The piece highlights UNESCO's role in shaping international AI governance frameworks in coordination with the G7.

Ongoing: Active Call and Upcoming Events

Open Call: Remote Access to AI Infrastructure (Deadline: August 31, 2026)

  • URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-and-ai-lab/cbpfs-call-remote-access-artificial-intelligence-infrastructure
  • UNESCO and Brazil's Computing and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (AI-Lab/CBPF) are offering researchers from underrepresented regions remote access to high-performance computing and AI infrastructure. The program targets MSc/PhD students, postdocs, and junior faculty from Latin America, Africa, Ukraine, and island nations, with applications due August 31, 2026.
