Environment October 3, 2026

UNESCO Environment Weekly Report 2026-10-03

Overview

During the week of September 26 – October 3, 2026, UNESCO's environment-related activities centered on climate education and water resource cooperation. UNESCO published an article on its Greening Education Partnership's upcoming role at COP31, where the partnership is set to reach 100 member countries. UNESCO also participated in the Euro-Mediterranean Water Forum, advancing water cooperation and resilience in the region.

UNESCO at COP31: Putting Education at the Heart of Climate Action

Date: 2 October 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-cop31-putting-education-heart-climate-action

UNESCO's Greening Education Partnership (GEP) is set to reach a key milestone at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, on 14 November 2026, when Türkiye will become its 100th member country. Launched at the 2022 UN Transforming Education Summit, the GEP has grown into the largest global alliance for climate education, bringing together 100 countries and more than 2,000 organizations. At COP31, UNESCO plans to launch GEP 2.0, which will feature enhanced country-level implementation frameworks and a new Monitoring and Evaluation Working Group.

UNESCO at the Euro-Mediterranean Water Forum: Advancing Water Cooperation and Resilience (event, article text unverified)

Date: 29 September – 2 October 2026 (event)
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/events/all

UNESCO participated in the Euro-Mediterranean Water Forum held September 29 – October 2, 2026, with a dedicated session on advancing water cooperation and resilience in the region. UNESCO's World Water Assessment Programme (WWAP) and Intergovernmental Hydrological Programme (IHP) support water management in the Euro-Mediterranean area. This event was confirmed via UNESCO's official calendar; a detailed article was not retrieved.
