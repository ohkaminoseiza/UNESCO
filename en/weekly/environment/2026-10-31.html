Environment October 31, 2026

UNESCO Environment Weekly Report 2026-10-31

Overview

During the week of October 24–31, 2026, three UNESCO environment-related developments were confirmed. A UN delegation visited the UNESCO-designated biosphere reserve in Tobago, UNESCO Peru launched a community preparedness campaign for the approaching El Niño phenomenon, and the IOC of UNESCO announced the rescheduled NEAMWave26 tsunami preparedness exercise covering the North-Eastern Atlantic, Mediterranean, and connected seas. All three reflect UNESCO's ongoing work on climate adaptation and disaster risk reduction.

UNESCO Biosphere Reserve Highlighted During UN Day Visit to Tobago

Date: 28 October 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-biosphere-reserve-highlighted-during-un-day-tobago

On 26 October 2026, a UN delegation visiting Tobago for United Nations Day toured the North-East Tobago Biosphere Reserve, designated in 2020 under UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere (MAB) Programme. The visit took place alongside a Development Partners' Meeting convened by the Tobago House of Assembly and the UN in Trinidad and Tobago, attended by representatives of 13 countries and nine UN agencies.

Bandiougou Diawara of UNESCO's Regional Office for the Caribbean highlighted the reserve's contribution to sustainable tourism and climate resilience, noting its coverage of terrestrial and marine ecosystems including the Main Ridge Forest Reserve, coral reefs, and mangroves. UNESCO also announced it is about to launch a multi-hazard risk assessment and mapping initiative for the reserve, which will inform disaster preparedness, ecosystem restoration, and tourism planning.

UNESCO Peru Launches El Niño Community Preparedness Campaign

Date: 30 October 2026
Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-promotes-prepared-resilient-and-organized-communities-face-el-nino

UNESCO Peru launched the Comunidades PRO (Prepared, Resilient and Organized Communities) campaign on 26 October 2026 at a women's football match between Alianza Lima and Talleres de Córdoba in Lima. The campaign responds to Peru's ENFEN commission forecast, which gives a high probability of an extraordinary El Niño between September 2026 and January 2027.

UNESCO is promoting Media and Information Literacy (MIL) to help people consult official sources and verify information before emergencies strike, and is co-designing a play-based toolkit with the education sector to sustain learning and protect children's social-emotional well-being during disasters. UNESCO is also working with Peru's Ministry of Culture and the civil defense institute INDECI to strengthen the capacity of cultural officials and municipalities to protect heritage. The campaign highlights women's central role in community organization and disaster response.

IOC of UNESCO Announces Rescheduled NEAMWave26 Tsunami Preparedness Exercise

Date: 30 October 2026
Source: https://www.ioc.unesco.org/en/articles/intergovernmental-oceanographic-commission-ioc-unesco-strengthening-tsunami-preparedness-rescheduled

The Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission of UNESCO announced that the NEAMWave26 regional tsunami exercise has been rescheduled to 5–19 November 2026, aligned with World Tsunami Awareness Day. The exercise covers the North-Eastern Atlantic, the Mediterranean, and connected seas, and tests early warning systems and emergency response, including the EU Civil Protection Mechanism through the European Commission's ERCC.

Five scenarios based on historical tsunamigenic earthquakes will run across the Ligurian Sea, Cyprian Arc, Ionian Sea, Northeast Atlantic (for testing international assistance), and Black Sea. Italy's INGV will for the first time apply its Probabilistic Tsunami Forecasting method to issue alerts in the joint Ligurian and Ionian Sea scenarios. The exercise also helps coastal communities meet UNESCO-IOC Tsunami Ready Indicator 8, which requires a community-based preparedness exercise at least every two years.
