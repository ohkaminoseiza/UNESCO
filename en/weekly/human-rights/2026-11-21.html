Human Rights November 21, 2026

UNESCO Human Rights Weekly Report 2026-11-21

Overview

During the week of November 14–21, 2026, UNESCO focused its human rights communications on the intersection of artificial intelligence and human rights, in the lead-up to the 80th anniversary of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. An article published on November 18 announced an upcoming high-level event in Paris examining how AI affects human rights, cultural diversity, social cohesion, and information integrity. The organization also marked International Tolerance Day (November 16) with the 15th ceremony of the UNESCO-Madanjeet Singh Prize.

Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights: Protecting Human Dignity in the Age of AI

Date: 18 November 2026

UNESCO, the European Union, and the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan announced they will jointly host a Human Rights Day 2026 event — "Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights" — at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris on Wednesday, 25 November 2026 (3:00–5:00 p.m., Room II). The programme includes a screening of The Beginning, an AI-produced film by Jordanian director Ibraheem Diab following a child fleeing conflict, and a high-level panel discussion on AI's role in peacebuilding and inclusion. The event will also launch the publication Human Rights: Challenging 13 Common Misconceptions, produced by UNESCO and the French National Consultative Commission on Human Rights (CNCDH) with EU support, while highlighting UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence as the first global standard-setting instrument guiding rights-based AI design and deployment.

Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/artificial-intelligence-and-human-rights-protecting-human-dignity-age-ai

UNESCO-Madanjeet Singh Prize for the Promotion of Tolerance and Non-Violence — 15th Ceremony

Date: Ceremony held on 16 November 2026 (International Day for Tolerance)

UNESCO held the 15th ceremony of the UNESCO-Madanjeet Singh Prize on 16 November 2026, the International Day for Tolerance. The prize, awarded every two years, carries a US$100,000 award to an individual or organization contributing to the promotion of tolerance and non-violence. As of the date this report was compiled (21 November 2026), the names of the 2026 laureates had not yet been published on the UNESCO website.

Source: https://www.unesco.org/en/prizes/madanjeet-singh
