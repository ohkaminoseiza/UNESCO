# UNESCO Environment Weekly Report 2026-06-13

## Overview

The week of June 6–13, 2026 was particularly active for UNESCO's environmental work. On World Environment Day (June 5), UNESCO announced the designation of 14 new biosphere reserves across 14 countries, expanding the World Network of Biosphere Reserves to 797 sites in 145 countries. The 38th session of the Man and the Biosphere (MAB) International Coordinating Council (MAB-ICC) was held in Paraguay's Itaipu Biosphere Reserve from June 3–6. Additional highlights include the recognition of Timor-Leste's first biosphere reserve (June 6), the designation of a Portuguese mountain range as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve (June 8), and the ongoing call for applications to the 2026 UNESCO Sultan Qaboos Prize for Environmental Conservation (deadline: June 15). UNESCO also reaffirmed its commitment to protecting World Heritage sites from the escalating impacts of climate change.

---

## UNESCO Designates 14 New Biosphere Reserves on World Environment Day

**Date:** June 5, 2026  
**Summary:** On World Environment Day 2026, UNESCO announced the designation of 14 new biosphere reserves in Albania, Algeria, Aruba, Azerbaijan, Cameroon, Canada, Iran, Mongolia, Montenegro, Paraguay, Philippines, Portugal, Timor-Leste, and Vietnam. The additions bring the World Network of Biosphere Reserves to 797 sites across 145 countries, covering approximately 10 million km² of land. Each site serves as a model for integrating biodiversity conservation with sustainable development and climate resilience, supporting local communities while protecting globally significant ecosystems.  
**Source:** [UNESCO designates 14 new biosphere reserves on World Environment Day | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-designates-14-new-biosphere-reserves-world-environment-day)

---

## 38th MAB International Coordinating Council Session Held in Paraguay

**Date:** June 3–6, 2026  
**Summary:** The 38th session of the International Coordinating Council of UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere (MAB) Programme was held at the Itaipu Biosphere Reserve in Paraguay, marking only the second time this body has convened in Latin America and the Caribbean. The council reviewed and approved the 14 new biosphere reserve designations, discussed strategic priorities for the MAB Programme, and reinforced the role of UNESCO-designated sites in addressing biodiversity loss and climate change. Delegations from Morocco, Japan, and numerous other Member States participated in the session.  
**Source:** [38th session of the International Coordinating Council of the Man and the Biosphere Programme | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/38th-session-international-coordinating-council-man-and-biosphere-programme)

## World Environment Day 2026: A Call for Climate Action

**Date:** June 5, 2026  
**Summary:** Under the theme "Climate Action," UNESCO marked World Environment Day 2026 by reaffirming the critical role of World Heritage sites in protecting the planet's natural and cultural treasures. UNESCO highlighted how climate change impacts — including coral bleaching, rapidly retreating glaciers, prolonged droughts, floods, and wildfires — are already affecting the world's most iconic World Heritage sites. The organization stressed that safeguarding cultural and natural heritage and safeguarding the environment are inseparable goals, and called for accelerated global action to reduce emissions and strengthen ecosystem resilience.  
**Source:** [World Environment Day 2026: A Call for Climate Action - UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/news/2900)

## UNESCO Recognizes Timor-Leste's First Biosphere Reserve: Nino Konis Santana National Park

**Date:** June 6, 2026  
**Summary:** UNESCO designated Nino Konis Santana National Park as Timor-Leste's first biosphere reserve, marking the country's formal entry into the World Network of Biosphere Reserves. The park encompasses unique terrestrial and marine ecosystems with significant biodiversity. The designation provides Timor-Leste with access to international expertise and support for sustainable land and sea management, strengthening conservation of its rich natural heritage while supporting local communities.  
**Source:** [UNESCO recognizes Nino Konis Santana park as Timor-Leste's first Biosphere Reserve](https://en.tatoli.tl/2026/06/06/unesco-recognizes-nino-konis-santana-park-as-timor-lestes-first-biosphere-reserve/23/)

## Portuguese Mountain Range Joins UNESCO Biosphere Reserve Network

**Date:** June 8, 2026  
**Summary:** A Portuguese mountain range was newly designated as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, recognizing the area's rich ecosystems, landscapes, and the cultural traditions of its local communities. The designation reflects UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere Programme's philosophy of promoting harmonious coexistence between humans and nature, integrating conservation with sustainable development. The new site adds to Portugal's existing portfolio of UNESCO-designated areas and strengthens European participation in the global biosphere reserve network.  
**Source:** [Portuguese mountain range joins UNESCO Biosphere Reserve](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-06-08/portuguese-mountain-range-joins-unesco-biosphere-reserve/1036278)

## Applications Open for 2026 UNESCO Sultan Qaboos Prize for Environmental Conservation (Deadline: June 15)

**Date:** June 2026 (deadline: June 15, 2026)  
**Summary:** UNESCO is accepting applications for the 2026 Sultan Qaboos Prize for Environmental Conservation, with the deadline set for June 15, 2026. The prize honors individuals, groups, institutions, or NGOs that have made outstanding contributions to environmental conservation. The award will be presented at the World Science Forum in November 2026. This biennial prize underscores UNESCO's commitment to recognizing excellence in protecting biodiversity, promoting sustainable resource use, and fostering innovative conservation solutions worldwide.  
**Source:** [Applications open for the 2026 UNESCO Sultan Qaboos Prize for Environmental Conservation | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/applications-open-2026-unesco-sultan-qaboos-prize-environmental-conservation)
