## UNESCO at COP31: Water and Ocean Resilience

**Date:** 9 November 2026 (COP31 runs 9–20 November 2026)  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/cop31-water

The 31st UN Climate Change Conference (COP31) opened in Antalya, Türkiye on 9 November 2026, running until 20 November, with water and ocean resilience as its central theme. UNESCO is advocating for integrated solutions that combine science, education, and the protection of local and Indigenous knowledge systems, while warning that the cryosphere—which stores nearly 70% of the world's freshwater—is melting at an accelerating rate and threatens water supplies and food systems. On 13–14 November, UNESCO's Greening Education Partnership holds its 4th annual meeting at COP31 to discuss next steps for monitoring and evaluation, with Türkiye set to become its 100th member country.
