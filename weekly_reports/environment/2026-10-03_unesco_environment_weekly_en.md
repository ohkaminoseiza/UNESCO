# UNESCO Environment Weekly Report 2026-10-03

## Overview

During the week of September 26 – October 3, 2026, UNESCO's environment-related activities centered on climate education and water resource cooperation. UNESCO also participated in the Euro-Mediterranean Water Forum, advancing water cooperation and resilience in the region.

## UNESCO at COP31: Putting Education at the Heart of Climate Action

**Date:** 2 October 2026
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-cop31-putting-education-heart-climate-action

UNESCO's Greening Education Partnership (GEP) is set to reach a key milestone at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, on 14 November 2026, when Türkiye will become its 100th member country. Launched at the 2022 UN Transforming Education Summit, the GEP has grown into the largest global alliance for climate education, bringing together 100 countries and more than 2,000 organizations. At COP31, UNESCO plans to launch GEP 2.0, which will feature enhanced country-level implementation frameworks and a new Monitoring and Evaluation Working Group.
