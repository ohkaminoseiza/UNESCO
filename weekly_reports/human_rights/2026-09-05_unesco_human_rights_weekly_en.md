# UNESCO Human Rights Weekly Report 2026-09-05

## Overview

This week (ending September 5, 2026), UNESCO's human rights activities were marked by significant international milestones. On September 4, the United Nations General Assembly proclaimed 2027–2036 as the International Decade of Culture for Sustainable Development, designating UNESCO as the lead implementing agency with an explicit emphasis on culture as a cornerstone of human rights and global solidarity. Also on September 4, UNESCO hosted events marking the International Day to Protect Education from Attack, highlighting the human right to education in conflict zones. Looking ahead, the International Literacy Day global ceremony is scheduled for September 8–9 in Mexico, affirming literacy as a fundamental human right.

---

## International Decade of Culture for Sustainable Development (2027–2036) Proclaimed

**Date:** September 4, 2026

The UN General Assembly adopted a resolution on September 4, 2026 proclaiming 2027–2036 as the International Decade of Culture for Sustainable Development, co-sponsored by 103 Member States and adopted 174 votes to 1. UNESCO will serve as the lead agency, developing an Action Plan to mobilize governments, organizations, and communities around leveraging culture as a driving force for inclusive, resilient, and sustainable societies. The proclamation explicitly frames culture as a cornerstone of multilateralism, global cooperation, solidarity, and respect for human rights.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/culture-sustainable-development-new-un-decade-renewed-global-ambition](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/culture-sustainable-development-new-un-decade-renewed-global-ambition)

## International Day to Protect Education from Attack: "The Crossing" Installation

**Date:** September 4–9, 2026

To mark the International Day to Protect Education from Attack (observed September 9), UNESCO hosted "The Crossing," an awareness installation at its Paris headquarters. Held under the theme "Can education survive attack? The resilience of human communities," the event aimed to inspire action to protect students, teachers, and education communities in conflict zones worldwide. The initiative underscores UNESCO's commitment to defending the right to education as a fundamental human right, even in the most challenging circumstances.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/events](https://www.unesco.org/en/events)

---

## International Literacy Day 2026 (September 8–9, Mexico)

**Date:** September 8–9, 2026 (upcoming)

UNESCO will convene the global International Literacy Day celebration on September 8–9 in Mexico, in collaboration with the Mexican government, featuring the UNESCO International Literacy Prizes award ceremony recognizing outstanding literacy programs from around the world. Literacy remains a critical human rights issue: approximately 765 million young people and adults currently lack basic literacy skills, two-thirds of whom are women. UNESCO's ongoing strategy addresses this gap by strengthening national literacy programs, prioritizing disadvantaged groups, and integrating digital technologies.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/days/literacy](https://www.unesco.org/en/days/literacy)
