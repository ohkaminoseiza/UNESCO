# UNESCO Human Rights Weekly Report 2026-10-03

## Overview

During the week of September 26 – October 3, 2026, UNESCO's activities related to human rights focused on journalist safety and the right to access information. The UNESCO Director-General condemned the killing of a veteran journalist and community radio director in Haiti. ## UNESCO Director-General Condemns the Killing of Journalist Joseph Willard Vancol in Haiti

**Date:** 29 September 2026
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-director-general-condemns-killing-journalist-joseph-willard-vancol-haiti

UNESCO Director-General Khaled El-Enany condemned the killing of Joseph Willard Vancol, a veteran journalist and director of community radio station Ti Boukan FM in Haiti, who was abducted by an armed gang on 20 August 2026 and found dead on 9 September 2026. El-Enany emphasized that "Community media play an essential role in promoting access to information" and called on Haitian authorities to thoroughly investigate the killing and bring the perpetrators to justice. The Director-General stressed that "Impunity for such crimes is unacceptable and must not be tolerated."
