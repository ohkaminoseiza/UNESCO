## 5th UNESCO Global Forum against Racism and Discrimination Convenes in The Hague

**Date:** 29–30 October 2026 (event dates; UNESCO article publication date not confirmed — article body not retrieved)
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/5th-unesco-global-forum-against-racism-and-discrimination *(article body not confirmed)*

The 5th UNESCO Global Forum against Racism and Discrimination was held on 29–30 October 2026 at World Forum The Hague, Netherlands. The forum is UNESCO's annual platform on this theme; the 2024 edition in Barcelona was described as the largest in the series. The 2026 edition adopted the theme "From commitment to accountability: change the system, change the future."

The event was organized by the Dutch National Coordinator against Discrimination and Racism (NCDR) in partnership with UNESCO and several Dutch ministries. According to the Dutch government, the King of the Netherlands attended the opening ceremony on 29 October, and the Prime Minister delivered the opening address. More than 1,500 participants from over 85 countries were reported to have attended.
