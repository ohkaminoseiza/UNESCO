# UNESCO Human Rights Weekly Report 2026-11-14

## Overview

During the week of 7–14 November 2026, UNESCO co-hosted a high-level dialogue with the European Commission at its Paris headquarters on addressing racism in and through education. The event marked a milestone in developing UNESCO's first policy guide on anti-racist education, with over 30 experts from across Europe gathering in a closed-door consultation on 12–13 November.

## High-Level Dialogue on Addressing Racism in and Through Education

**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/how-build-anti-racist-education-systems-high-level-dialogue-addressing-racism-and-through-education

UNESCO, in partnership with the European Commission, hosted a high-level dialogue titled "How to build anti-racist education systems?" on 12 November 2026 at its Paris headquarters. The event brought together policymakers, educators, experts, and young people to explore how education systems can more effectively address racism, and it marks a milestone in developing UNESCO's first policy guide on the topic. A closed-door expert consultation with over 30 experts from across Europe also took place on 12–13 November to inform the forthcoming guide.
