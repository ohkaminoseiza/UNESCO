# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-06-27

## Overview

During the week of June 20–27, 2026, UNESCO actively advanced its artificial intelligence agenda with a focus on AI governance and ethics. The week's biggest highlight was the Third Ministerial and High-Level Authorities Summit on the Ethics of AI in Latin America and the Caribbean, held on June 25–26 in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, with representatives from more than 20 countries. Further activities included UNESCO's participation in the India AI Impact Summit championing ethical, human-centered AI; final preparations for the Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (July 6–7); and the launch of the second cohort of UNESCO's rights-based data governance programme for policymakers. UNESCO continues to exercise international leadership in promoting the implementation of its 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence.

---

## Third Ministerial and High-Level Authorities Summit on the Ethics of AI in Latin America and the Caribbean

**Date:** June 25–26, 2026  
**Summary:** The Third Ministerial and High-Level Authorities Summit on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence was held in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic — the region's main platform for political dialogue on AI development and responsible governance. Co-organized by UNESCO, the Dominican Republic Government Office of Information and Communication Technologies (OGTIC), and CAF – Development Bank of Latin America and the Caribbean, the summit brought together ministers, senior officials, multilateral organizations, academia, the private sector, and civil society from more than 20 countries. The event aimed to strengthen regional cooperation and accelerate the implementation of public policies aligned with the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, with country-level progress on AI ethics frameworks shared among participants.  
**Source:** [The Third Ministerial Summit on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/third-ministerial-summit-ethics-artificial-intelligence-latin-america-and-caribbean-takes-place)

## India AI Impact Summit: UNESCO Champions Ethical and Human-Centered AI

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO participated in the India AI Impact Summit, advocating for ethical and human-centered approaches to artificial intelligence. Drawing on its landmark 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI — applicable to all 194 UNESCO Member States — the Organization stressed the importance of governance frameworks that ensure AI contributes to social inclusion, human rights, and sustainable development. The summit facilitated dialogue with AI policymakers from India and other emerging economies, reinforcing UNESCO's role as a global convener on AI ethics.  
**Source:** [India AI Impact Summit: UNESCO champions ethical and human-centered AI | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/india-ai-impact-summit-unesco-champions-ethical-and-human-centered-ai)

## Governments Advance Rights-Based Data Governance to Unlock Inclusive AI Futures

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO launched the second cohort of its rights-based data governance programme for policymakers, expanding structured guidance on designing data governance systems that protect citizens' rights in the age of AI. Building on the outcomes of the first cohort, the programme helps governments implement policies that safeguard privacy, prevent discrimination, and ensure accountability in data-driven systems. Participating policymakers receive practical tools and frameworks grounded in international human rights standards.  
**Source:** [Governments advance rights-based data governance to unlock inclusive AI futures | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/governments-advance-rights-based-data-governance-unlock-inclusive-ai-futures)

---

## Policy Dialogue on AI Governance

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO continued its policy dialogue on AI governance in preparation for the Global Dialogue on AI Governance, scheduled for July 6–7 in Geneva, which UNESCO and the ITU are co-hosting as the joint UN Secretariat. The dialogue aims to build consensus on AI governance frameworks that reflect the priorities of all nations, not only the most technologically advanced. It will be held alongside the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) Forum 2026 (July 6–10) and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit (July 7–10).  
**Source:** [Policy Dialogue on AI Governance | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/policy-dialogue-ai-governance)
