### Pilot Program Launches in Mexico to Co-Build a Model of Centers of Excellence Between UNESCO, CONALEP, and DGETI

- **Date:** August 18, 2026
- **URL:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/pilot-program-launches-mexico-co-build-model-centers-excellence-between-unesco-conalep-and-dgeti
- UNESCO, CONALEP, and DGETI launched a pilot initiative at eight technical education campuses in Mexico's Valley of Mexico region for 2026–2027. The program establishes innovation hubs to strengthen vocational education through digital talent development and teacher training. Lessons learned will inform scaling the model across Latin America and the Caribbean.
