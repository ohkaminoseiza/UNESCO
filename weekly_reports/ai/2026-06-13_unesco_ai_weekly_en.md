# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-06-13

## Overview

During the week of June 6–13, 2026, UNESCO advanced several significant artificial intelligence initiatives. Key activities included the launch of the first sub-national AI Readiness Assessment Report for Flanders (Belgium) on June 2, the opening of the second cohort of the joint UNESCO-UNDP data governance capacity-building course, the release of French and Spanish editions of the free "AI and Rule of Law" online course, and a panel discussion on new technology challenges held in partnership with ULACIT (Costa Rica) on June 12. Preparations are also underway for the upcoming Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (July 6–7, 2026), with early registration now strongly recommended.

---

## First Local AI Readiness Assessment Report Launched in Flanders

**Date:** June 2, 2026  
**Summary:** UNESCO launched the first sub-national AI Readiness Assessment Report for the Flanders region of Belgium at Digitaal Vlaanderen's AI Inspiratie Dag. Flanders is the first regional government to apply UNESCO's Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM) at the sub-national level, and one of the first to pilot the new RAM 2.0 framework. The report identifies policy gaps and provides a strategic foundation for Flanders to develop its regional AI policy and strategy in alignment with UNESCO's ethical guidelines.  
**Source:** [First Local Readiness Assessment Report Launched in Flanders | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/first-local-readiness-assessment-report-launched-flanders)

---

## UNESCO-UNDP Joint Data Governance Course: Second Cohort Launched

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) launched the second cohort of their joint capacity-building initiative, "Data Governance for Inclusive Digital and AI Futures," hosted at the ITU Academy with support from the EU Global Gateway initiative. The course equips government officials with practical tools to design rights-based data governance frameworks that support inclusive digital and AI futures. The second cohort expands access to structured, hands-on guidance for policymakers worldwide.  
**Source:** [Governments advance rights-based data governance to unlock inclusive AI futures | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/governments-advance-rights-based-data-governance-unlock-inclusive-ai-futures)

---

## Free Global Course on "AI and Rule of Law" Now Available in French and Spanish

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO and the University of Oxford released French and Spanish language editions of their free global online course on "Artificial Intelligence and the Rule of Law." The course helps learners worldwide understand how AI affects justice systems, human rights, and the rule of law. By adding these language editions, UNESCO broadens access to AI literacy and legal understanding for policymakers, civil society, and students across the Global South and Francophone and Hispanophone regions.  
**Source:** [UNESCO and University of Oxford launch a free global course on AI and rule of law | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-and-university-oxford-launch-free-global-course-ai-and-rule-law)

---

## UNESCO-ULACIT Panel: "Who Serves Whom? The Challenges of New Technologies"

**Date:** June 12, 2026  
**Summary:** UNESCO partnered with ULACIT (Latin American University of Science and Technology, Costa Rica) to host the panel discussion "Who Serves Whom? The Challenges of New Technologies." The event brought together experts to examine the societal, ethical, and human-rights dimensions of AI and emerging technologies in the Latin American context. The discussion contributed to raising awareness of AI ethics and governance challenges in Central America and the broader region.  
**Source:** [AI Day 2026 at UNESCO: Youth and Digital Transformation | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-day-2026-unesco-youth-and-digital-transformation)

---

## Registration Open: Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, July 6–7

**Date:** June 2026 (this week)  
**Summary:** UNESCO and the ITU, as joint UN Secretariat for the Global Dialogue on AI Governance, have opened early registration, strongly recommended by June 25, 2026. The Dialogue will take place in Geneva alongside the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) Forum 2026 (July 6–10) and ITU's AI for Good Global Summit (July 7–10). The event will bring together Member States and stakeholders to discuss how AI governance frameworks can reflect the priorities of all nations, not just the most technologically advanced, and is expected to shape multilateral AI policy.  
**Source:** [Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, 6–7 July | UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-dialogue-ai-governance-geneva-6-7-july)
