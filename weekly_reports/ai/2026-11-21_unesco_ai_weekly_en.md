# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-11-21

## Overview

During the week of November 14–21, 2026, UNESCO continued publishing on AI in education and data governance. Building on its Digital Learning Week 2026, UNESCO highlighted the potential of small, task-specific AI models ("frugal AI") to narrow learning gaps in low-resource settings. The organization also reported on its work to strengthen data governance in Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) in support of ethical AI deployment.

## Small AI models widen access to learning in low-resource settings

**Date:** 17 November 2026 (updated 18 November 2026)

UNESCO reported on discussions at Digital Learning Week 2026 in Paris showing that compact, task-specific AI models can outperform large language models for bounded learning tasks while running on inexpensive devices with limited or no internet access. Practical examples included EIDU's remedial-lesson AI used on shared classroom devices in around 14,000 public schools in Kenya and Nigeria, an offline essay-scoring app in Brazil where an average of 29 pupils share one school computer, and Cambridge's prototype maths tutor running on a mid-range phone without internet. A joint UNESCO–University College London report, *Smarter, Smaller, Stronger*, estimates that smaller, task-specific models can cut energy use by up to 90% while maintaining strong performance.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/small-ai-models-widen-access-learning-low-resource-settings

## UNESCO promotes stronger data governance in Latin America and the Caribbean

**Date:** 16 November 2026

UNESCO participated in the Second High-Level Policy Dialogue on Data Governance, an EU–LAC event in Madrid attended by more than 100 representatives from 22 LAC countries and 8 EU Member States. UNESCO Programme Specialist Leona Verdadero spoke on personal data and AI, presenting the organization's Data Governance Toolkit and its position that protecting personal data and enabling innovation are complementary objectives. UNESCO plans to continue supporting LAC countries through training, shared tools, and improved data quality, drawing on its Roadmap for Ethical AI in LAC 2026–2027 and Caribbean AI Policy Roadmap while respecting each nation's legislative autonomy.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-promotes-stronger-data-governance-latin-america-and-caribbean
