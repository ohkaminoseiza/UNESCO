# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-09-19

## Overview

The week of September 12–19, 2026 was dominated by UNESCO's 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (GFEAI 2026), held September 14–17 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The forum drew over 6,300 participants from more than 50 Member States and marked five years since UNESCO's landmark 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI. Key outcomes included the launch of updated governance tools — RAM 2.0 and an AI environmental impact toolkit — alongside a reaffirmed call for human-centered, inclusive AI governance globally. UNESCO also represented these priorities at the 81st UN General Assembly (UNGA81) and held educational events on AI at its Paris headquarters.

---

## 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of AI in Saudi Arabia

**Date:** September 18, 2026 (event: September 14–17, 2026)
**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-cooperation-ethical-ai-governance-reinforced-unescos-4th-global-forum-ethics-ai-saudi-arabia](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-cooperation-ethical-ai-governance-reinforced-unescos-4th-global-forum-ethics-ai-saudi-arabia)

UNESCO and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (via SDAIA and ICAIRE) co-hosted the 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of AI in Riyadh, under the theme "Transforming Global Cooperation for Ethical AI Governance." The forum, held alongside the Global AI Summit 2026, gathered approximately 8,000 participants including ministers and vice-ministers from around 50 countries. Key new tools were launched: RAM 2.0 (an updated AI Readiness Assessment Methodology incorporating perspectives from women, persons with disabilities, and indigenous peoples) and an AI environmental impact toolkit guiding policymakers on reducing AI's ecological footprint. The event also featured thematic sessions on agentic AI, gender equality, cultural implications, and young people's mental health, marking five years of progress under UNESCO's 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI.

## Learning with New Technologies and Artificial Intelligence

**Date:** September 16, 2026
**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/learning-new-technologies-and-artificial-intelligence](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/learning-new-technologies-and-artificial-intelligence)

UNESCO hosted a campus event for approximately 700 students at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris to explore how AI and digital technologies are reshaping educational practices. Expert panelists discussed the importance of preserving critical thinking and human effort in the learning process, cautioning against delegating intellectual work entirely to AI tools, and addressed the challenge of combating misinformation on social media. The event emphasized that "the future of education will not be written by algorithms alone," with partners including TECH4ALL (Huawei), IIIA-CSIC, and the Laboratory for Innovation and Digital Technology in Education (LINE).

## UNESCO Calls for Global Cooperation on AI and Ocean Science at UNGA81

**Date:** September 18, 2026
**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-calls-global-cooperation-address-current-global-challenges-artificial-intelligence-and-ocean](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-calls-global-cooperation-address-current-global-challenges-artificial-intelligence-and-ocean)

During the 81st UN General Assembly High-Level Week, UNESCO represented multilateral AI governance and ocean science priorities. UNESCO co-chaired the UN Dialogue on AI Governance and emphasized that "the development of AI models must serve human intelligence, human dignity and human agency — not weaken or replace them." The organization also signed cooperation agreements with the UNFCCC and UNAOC to strengthen climate action and education, and launched the ONE OCEAN exhibition promoting ocean literacy as part of its UNGA participation.

## UNESCO-Uzbekistan Beruniy Prize Laureates Highlight Ethics of AI in Kazakhstan

**Date:** September 18, 2026
**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-uzbekistan-beruniy-prize-laureates-highlight-ethics-ai-kazakhstan](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-uzbekistan-beruniy-prize-laureates-highlight-ethics-ai-kazakhstan)

Laureates of the UNESCO-Uzbekistan Beruniy Prize participated in an event in Kazakhstan highlighting the ethical dimensions of AI development and deployment. The activity reinforced UNESCO's ongoing work on ethics in science and technology across Central Asia, building regional capacity to engage with AI governance frameworks aligned with the 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI.
