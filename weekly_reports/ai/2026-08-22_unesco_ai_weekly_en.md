# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-08-22

## Overview

During the week of August 15–22, 2026, UNESCO advanced several significant initiatives in the field of artificial intelligence. The most notable development within the strict 7-day window was the launch of an AI-focused Centers of Excellence pilot program in Mexico on August 18. Earlier in the month, a major international seminar on AI, human rights, and the rule of law held in London released findings (August 5). Preparations are also accelerating for the 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (GFEAI 2026), scheduled for September 14–17 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, signaling a growing global momentum toward ethical AI governance frameworks.

---

## Pilot Program Launches in Mexico to Co-Build a Model of Centers of Excellence Between UNESCO, CONALEP, and DGETI

**Date:** August 18, 2026  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/pilot-program-launches-mexico-co-build-model-centers-excellence-between-unesco-conalep-and-dgeti

UNESCO launched a pilot initiative in Mexico in partnership with technical education institutions CONALEP and DGETI, targeting eight campuses in the Valley of Mexico for the 2026–2027 period. The program aims to strengthen technical and vocational education by boosting digital talent and introducing innovative teaching methods that incorporate responsible AI. Results from this initiative will inform efforts to scale the model across Latin America and the Caribbean.

---

## AI, Human Rights and Rule of Law: A Global Debate in London

**Date:** August 5, 2026  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-human-rights-and-rule-law-global-debate-london

Over 65 countries' legal professionals convened at a seminar in London on June 20 to examine artificial intelligence's growing role in justice systems. Participants concluded that "AI can genuinely strengthen justice systems, but only if human rights, judicial independence and access to justice are built into its design from the start." The event highlighted risks of over-relying on AI tools without proper judicial training and underscored the importance of civil society oversight.

## 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of AI to be Held in Riyadh (September 14–17)

**Date:** Ongoing (announced as of August 2026)  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/forum-ethics-ai

UNESCO and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — represented by SDAIA and ICAIRE — will co-host the 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (GFEAI 2026) in Riyadh from September 14 to 17, 2026. Under the theme "Transforming Global Cooperation for Ethical AI Governance," the forum will bring together all UN member states, the private sector, academia, and civil society to advance international AI governance frameworks.

---

## UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance Launches with Call for Inclusive, Safe AI

**Date:** July 6, 2026  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/un-global-dialogue-opens-urgent-call-safe-and-inclusive-ai-benefits-all

The United Nations launched its inaugural Global Dialogue on AI Governance, convening governments, technology companies, academia, and civil society to build frameworks ensuring AI benefits all nations equitably. The dialogue addresses critical themes including safety, accountability, human oversight, and bridging the "AI divide" between technologically advanced and developing countries. UN Secretary-General António Guterres emphasized that this platform represents a historic shift: "The question is whether we will govern it together — or let it govern us."
