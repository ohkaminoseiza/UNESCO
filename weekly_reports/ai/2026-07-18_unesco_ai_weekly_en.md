# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-07-18

## Overview

During the week of 11–18 July 2026, UNESCO advanced multiple significant AI governance and ethics initiatives. Outcome reporting from the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance (Geneva, 6–7 July) highlighted an urgent international call for safe and inclusive AI. The first Global Conference of the International Decade of Sciences for Sustainable Development (IDSSD), held at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris on 15–17 July, featured "Harnessing AI for Sustainable Development" as a key thematic session. A landmark joint report by the Thomson Reuters Foundation and UNESCO also shed new light on how 3,000 companies worldwide are approaching AI strategy and governance.

---

## UN Global Dialogue Opens with Urgent Call for Safe and Inclusive AI

**Date:** 6–7 July 2026 (outcomes reported from 11 July onward)

The inaugural session of the UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance opened in Geneva, co-coordinated by UNESCO and the ITU as the joint UN Secretariat, mandated by the UN General Assembly. The Dialogue brought together governments, technology companies, academia, civil society, and the technical community to ensure AI governance reflects the priorities of all nations — not just the most technologically advanced ones. Key discussion themes included the opportunities and implications of AI, bridging the AI divide, international cooperation on AI governance, and robust human oversight of AI systems.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/un-global-dialogue-opens-urgent-call-safe-and-inclusive-ai-benefits-all

## Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, 6–7 July

**Date:** 6–7 July 2026

Held alongside the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) Forum 2026 (6–10 July) and the ITU AI for Good Global Summit (7–10 July), the Dialogue's inaugural session in Geneva marked a milestone in global multilateral AI governance. UNESCO and the ITU jointly coordinate the Dialogue, which seeks to ensure that the benefits of AI are shared equitably and that governance frameworks align with the needs and realities of all member states. Discussions addressed AI's societal implications, the international AI governance landscape, and calls for meaningful inclusion of developing nations in governance processes.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/global-dialogue-ai-governance-geneva-6-7-july

## IDSSD Global Conference: "Harnessing AI for Sustainable Development" Session

**Date:** 15–17 July 2026

The first Global Conference of the International Decade of Sciences for Sustainable Development (IDSSD, 2024–2033), a hybrid event at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris, featured a dedicated parallel thematic session on "Harnessing AI for Sustainable Development" on 16–17 July. Under the conference theme "Science in action: Charting a sustainable and equitable future for all," participants explored how AI can accelerate progress on climate change, biodiversity, water security, and the Sustainable Development Goals. The conference highlighted that the global scientific community has rallied nearly 400 endorsed initiatives from 79 countries and mobilized USD 50 million in support of sciences for sustainable development.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/2026-global-conference-international-decade-sciences-sustainable-development-2024-2033

## Pioneering Report: Thomson Reuters Foundation and UNESCO Shed Light on How 3,000 Companies Approach AI

**Date:** July 2026 (exact date unconfirmed)

A pioneering joint report by the Thomson Reuters Foundation and UNESCO examined AI strategy adoption among 3,000 companies globally. Key findings reveal that 44% of companies surveyed have an AI strategy, while one in ten is publicly committed to adhering to an AI governance framework. The report also noted that, as of October 2025, over 75 countries from five continents have initiated or completed UNESCO's Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM) assessments, a tool supporting countries in translating UNESCO's AI Ethics Recommendation into national policy action.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/pioneering-report-thomson-reuters-foundation-and-unesco-sheds-light-way-3000-companies-approach-ai

## 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of AI Announced for September 2026, Riyadh

**Date:** July 2026 (announcement date unconfirmed)

UNESCO and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia announced the 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, scheduled for 14–17 September 2026 in Riyadh. The Forum will convene under the theme "Transforming Global Cooperation for Ethical AI Governance," building on UNESCO's 2021 Recommendation on the Ethics of AI — the first global normative instrument on AI ethics, applicable to all 194 UNESCO Member States. The forum is expected to assess implementation progress and chart pathways for strengthened international cooperation on AI ethics.

**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/forum-ethics-ai
