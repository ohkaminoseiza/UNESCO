# UNESCO AI Weekly Report 2026-09-05

## Overview

This week (ending September 5, 2026), UNESCO's AI-related activities centered on the practical implementation of AI governance frameworks and the promotion of global dialogue on ethical AI. A highlight was Suriname's launch of UNESCO's updated Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM 2.0) for responsible AI governance (September 2). A roundtable on designing agentic AI that respects cultural and epistemic diversity was also held (September 2). Looking ahead, the 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of AI is scheduled for September 14–17 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and Digital Learning Week at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris opens September 8–11, with AI in education as a central theme.

---

## Suriname Advances Responsible AI Governance with UNESCO's RAM 2.0

**Date:** September 2, 2026

Suriname became the first English- and Dutch-speaking Caribbean nation to undertake UNESCO's updated Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM 2.0) for artificial intelligence governance. The August 27, 2026 stakeholder meeting convened 106 participants from 67 institutions across 10 sectors to evaluate the country's current standing in areas including governance, regulation, education, and infrastructure regarding ethical AI adoption and development. This process marks a critical step in Suriname's efforts to build a national AI policy framework aligned with UNESCO ethical standards.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/suriname-advances-responsible-ai-governance-unescos-ram-20](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/suriname-advances-responsible-ai-governance-unescos-ram-20)

## "Plural Intelligences": Designing Agentic AI that Respects Cultural and Epistemic Diversity

**Date:** September 2, 2026

This UNESCO Business Council roundtable examined how AI agent systems can incorporate diverse worldviews and knowledge systems rather than homogenizing them. The discussion explored the implications of agentic AI for creativity, cultural production, and knowledge creation, drawing on UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence principles including inclusiveness and respect for different epistemologies. Participants considered concrete design approaches to ensure AI systems remain pluralistic and culturally aware.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/plural-intelligences-designing-agentic-ai-respects-cultural-and-epistemic-diversity](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/plural-intelligences-designing-agentic-ai-respects-cultural-and-epistemic-diversity)

## 4th Global Forum on the Ethics of AI (September 14–17, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia)

**Date:** Upcoming event (September 14–17, 2026)

The 4th UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence (GFEAI 2026) will take place from September 14 to 17 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, co-hosted with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) and the International Centre for AI Research and Ethics (ICAIRE). Operating under the theme "Transforming Global Cooperation for Ethical AI Governance," the forum will bring together ministers, AI experts, and civil society representatives to translate ethical principles into practical governance. Key topics include AI readiness and institutional capacity, AI supervision and accountability, gender equality and inclusion, health, youth well-being, cultural diversity, neurotechnology, sustainability, and AI safety.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/forum-ethics-ai](https://www.unesco.org/en/forum-ethics-ai)

## Digital Learning Week 2026 (September 8–11, Paris)

**Date:** Upcoming event (September 8–11, 2026)

UNESCO's Digital Learning Week 2026 will be held at UNESCO Headquarters in Paris under the title "Education in the age of AI: Facts, frictions and frontiers." The event brings together ministers, researchers, and educators to discuss the current state and future direction of AI in education, from policy frameworks to classroom implementation. The week will include ministerial-level dialogues, expert sessions, and the presentation of UNESCO's latest reports on AI's impact on learning environments globally.

**Source:** [https://www.unesco.org/en/weeks/digital-learning](https://www.unesco.org/en/weeks/digital-learning)
